Welcome to the Arnprior Board Gamers Community Group

We are just a bunch of people from Arnprior, and surrounding communities, that get together once week to play a variety of board games. There is usually a small games library available and people are encouraged to bring games they want to play.

We are ready to guide and teach, so their is no pressure if you haven't played many hobby board games before. You will find a mix from introductory to party to heavy strategy and more to discover.

New friends and great memories wait for you.
